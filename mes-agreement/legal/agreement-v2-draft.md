# Madhav Ecom Solution — Seller Agreement v2.0 (DRAFT — legal review required)

**Parties:** Madhav Ecom Solution ("MES") and the seller identified in the electronic enrollment record ("Seller").

**Duration:** 12 months beginning on the date both parties accept/sign this agreement, unless terminated earlier under its terms. Renewal requires a new recorded acceptance. For an existing signed seller, an addendum changes commission prospectively but does not restart or extend the original 12-month term.

**Services:** Marketplace listing, catalog optimization, seller operations, growth support and order/return review for the selected marketplaces, subject to marketplace rules.

**Fee:** MES charges INR 10 for each unique marketplace order ID successfully delivered to the buyer on or after the Seller's accepted effective date. Fee is per order, not per unit, package, shipment or item. Orders cancelled before delivery and RTO orders not delivered to the buyer incur no fee.

**Returns and refunds:** A complete return or full refund of a previously delivered order produces one INR 10 reversal/credit. A partial return does not reverse the fee if any part of the order remains fulfilled. Exchanges/replacements do not incur a second fee for the same original order; genuinely separate marketplace orders require reconciliation to prevent double charging.

**Records:** MES may enter delivered/return events manually using seller-provided marketplace order IDs, marketplace, event dates and status. The Seller may request reconciliation of discrepancies. Reversals offset unpaid charges and then subsequent invoices or are settled by mutual agreement.

**Payment and taxes:** Fees are reconciled periodically and payable as stated in the seller settlement statement. Applicable taxes, if any, are additional only when legally required and clearly invoiced. There is no fixed monthly service fee or minimum fee.

**Other terms:** Seller retains responsibility for lawful products, accurate account data, fulfillment, taxes and marketplace obligations. MES cannot guarantee sales volume or marketplace outcomes. Confidentiality, account access, notices, dispute resolution and termination remain subject to the separately reviewed general agreement clauses.

**Electronic acceptance:** The signed agreement must record seller identity, complete versioned terms, acceptance timestamp, signatory, effective date, and tamper-evident PDF/hash. Neither acceptance nor its evidence may be fabricated or backdated.

**Effective date:** New sellers: no earlier than completion of agreement signing and activation of the new model. Existing sellers: no earlier than separately signed addendum acceptance. Legacy 5% liabilities and signed PDFs remain unchanged.

## Existing signed seller — Commission Addendum v2.0 (DRAFT)

This addendum supplements the existing Seller Growth & Marketplace Services Agreement. The parties agree that **from the effective date recorded upon acceptance**, the fee clause is replaced prospectively by the INR 10 per unique successfully delivered marketplace order model, with INR 10 credit on full return/full refund, zero for cancelled-before-delivery/RTO, no duplicate charge on replacement, and no reversal for a partial return where any item remains fulfilled. Charges earned before this effective date remain governed by the original 5% Net Sales clause. All other clauses, **including the original 12-month expiry date**, remain unchanged. This addendum becomes effective only upon recorded acceptance by the Seller and MES.

**Implementation requirement:** Do not activate new billing for any seller until the full text/version, acceptance evidence and effective date are stored server-side. Obtain qualified Indian legal review before publication.
